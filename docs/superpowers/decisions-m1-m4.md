# Decisions taken on the owner's behalf, M1 to M4

This is the record of every ruling (a decision on a conflict, an ambiguity or a defect of the plan, with what it costs if it was wrong) and every deferred minor (a finding that was left alone on purpose) from the ledgers of the four stages of part 1. The ledgers are scratch files under `.superpowers/sdd/`, which git ignores, so the lines are copied here word for word, in the order they were written. A line can hold several rulings and a minor. The text is in English because the ledgers are.

Tag, release and publishing were never decided by the executor: they always need the owner's go-ahead, and none was given.

## M1: the task core (plan 2026-10-15-m1-task-core)

**How it was built:** Built test-first, every task gated by the whole suite and mutation-checked. A fresh reviewer read the whole stage; its Important findings (I1-I4) were fixed test-first (see the "Final (M1): fixed" lines in the ledger).
